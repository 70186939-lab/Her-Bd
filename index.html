My dearest Rumaisa,

Today the world got the gift of you, and somehow I was lucky enough to be standing close when it happened. I've tried to write this letter a hundred times. Every version felt too small, because there is no sentence big enough to hold what you are to me.

You are my best friend, the one person I can be completely myself with. You are also the love of my life, the one my heart chose without asking permission and has never once regretted. When you laugh, the day fixes itself. When you're quiet, I just want to sit next to you until you feel okay again.

Without you, I am incomplete. Not in a dramatic way, but in the quiet, honest way that a house is incomplete without light. I need you the way I need air, the way I need prayer. You are not a part of my life, Rumi. You are the reason the rest of it makes sense.

I'm sorry for every time I didn't say enough. I love you in ways I fumble to explain. I love your heart, your softness, your strength, even the days you doubt yourself. Especially those days, because I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even for a minute.

So here is my promise on your birthday: I will keep choosing you. In the easy days and the heavy ones. In every year Allah writes for us. And I will keep praying, every day, that you never know a sadness that lasts, that your heart is kept safe, and that I can spend the rest of my life making you feel cherished.

Happy Birthday, my sweet girl. I love you more than all the stars in the night sky.

Forever & Always,
Ahmad 🖤
